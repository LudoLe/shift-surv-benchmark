Across the $1692$ configurations, $1120$ were uninformative and $429$ were
supported. Fifty-nine configurations reached the threshold for a candidate
counterexample and were re-run on twenty seeds disjoint from the screening set.
Fourteen reproduced. Every one of them lies where ERM ranks target patients
below chance, and the method that gains there stays below $0.5$ AUROC after the
gain, reaching at most $0.456$: the improvement moves a model from clearly wrong
towards uninformative rather than towards useful. Of the candidates in which ERM
ranked above chance, none reproduced. The largest screening gain of $+0.113$
fell to $+0.022$ with an interval covering zero, and the rest fell below
$+0.021$. Across the pre-specified parameter space, with every method selected
per run, no adaptation gain survives confirmation.

The two designs behave differently, and the difference is itself informative.
Varying one parameter at a time around the benchmark configuration produces
$492$ settings, one candidate counterexample, and a largest gain of $+0.033$;
that candidate did not reproduce. The Latin hypercube design, which moves the
parameters together, produces every confirmed candidate. A single-parameter
perturbation therefore understates how far the conclusion can be pushed, and
reporting it alone would have been the weaker check.

The calibration results reproduce the pattern of the main experiments. The
directional rule held: prior adjustment improved ECE in $86$ of $100$
configurations under isolated label-prior shift with one source and $87$ of
$100$ with three, and degraded calibration under every other mechanism. The
effects reported for the remaining methods also reappeared across the space,
with Platt scaling the most consistent at one source, in $82$ and $62$
configurations of $100$ under concept and measurement shift, and the invariance
penalties the most consistent at three, IRM improving ECE in $74$ and $71$ of
$100$ under baseline-hazard and concept shift. No qualitative conclusion of the
main experiments changed across the pre-specified parameter space.
