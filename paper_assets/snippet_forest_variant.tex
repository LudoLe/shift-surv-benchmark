\caption{Real-data transfer results, each method versus ERM (positive is
better), pooled across centre transfers. \textbf{(a)} AUROC gap and
\textbf{(b)} ECE gain, each with the I3LUNG and registry cohorts as separate
blocks on a shared axis. Colour marks the method family, orange for adaptation,
gold for post-hoc recalibration and green for domain generalization; each
method is also named on the axis, so colour is redundant. No method recovers
discrimination on either cohort, IRM is significantly worse on I3LUNG, and
recalibration does not recover calibration. Domain generalization does not
apply to the registry, which has two centres. Points, random-effects pooled
estimate; bars, 95\% confidence interval. On I3LUNG, adaptation and
recalibration are pooled over the seven centre-to-centre transfers, IRM and
GroupDRO over the six leave-one-centre-out folds.}
\label{fig:real_res}
