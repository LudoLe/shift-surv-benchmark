\subsection{Sensitivity to the generator configuration}
\label{sec:sensitivity}

Every result so far is conditional on one generator configuration. A benchmark
whose conclusion holds only at the settings its authors chose measures those
settings, so we treat the configuration itself as something to vary.

\textbf{What varies.}
Eight parameters are declared in a configuration file frozen before any
sensitivity run: the number of patients per center, the observed dimension
$p$, the counts of invariant and spurious latent factors, the baseline hazard
$\lambda_0$, the Weibull shape $\nu$, the observation noise $\sigma$, and the
norm and direction of $w$. Three of them are declared not by their numerical
value but by the quantity they control, and calibrated to it on a fixed
super-population of $10^5$ subjects: $\lambda_0$ to a target event prevalence,
$\|w\|$ to a target latent-oracle AUROC, and $\sigma$ to a target
observed-oracle AUROC. A range of $\lambda_0$ means nothing to a reader; a
range of minority-class fractions spanning the $[0.16, 0.42]$ observed across
the six I3LUNG centers does. Severity is stratified alongside the generator
parameters, one column per regime, so a world is a complete specification of
both the data-generating process and how hard the shift is.

\textbf{Two designs.}
A one-at-a-time design moves each axis through its declared levels with the
others at their defaults, which isolates the effect of each parameter and is
readable. A space-filling design draws worlds from a Latin hypercube over all
axes jointly, which is the only one of the two that can find an interaction:
a conclusion can survive every axis moved alone and fail where two move
together. The direction of $w$ is drawn uniformly on the sphere rather than
stratified, a direction not being a scalar. Joint draws can leave the
overcompleteness premise the benchmark rests on --- $k_{\text{inv}}+k_{\text{spu}}
\le p/2$ --- and those worlds are clamped back onto it rather than dropped, so
the hypercube keeps all its rows; the adjustment is recorded per world.

\textbf{The rule, fixed in advance.}
Each configuration is screened on ten seeds and classified by the confidence
interval of the gain of the best adaptation method over ERM, not by its point
estimate: at ten seeds that gain carries a standard error near $0.005$, so
isolated configurations cross the $0.02$ margin by chance alone. A
configuration is \emph{supported} when the interval lies below the margin,
\emph{inconclusive} when it straddles it, a \emph{break} when it lies above,
and \emph{uninformative} when the observed-data oracle leaves less headroom
than the margin --- there, no method could exceed ERM by $0.02$ whatever it
did, and reporting it as support would be counting a vacuous case as evidence.
Every break and near-break is then re-run on seeds 100--119, disjoint from the
screening seeds, because taking the maximum over hundreds of noisy estimates
guarantees finding some above any threshold.

\textbf{Result.}
Of [N] configurations evaluated, [N] are uninformative in the sense above ---
a consequence of a deliberately wide sampling space, not of a defect --- and of
the remainder, [N] are supported. Five of the six regimes produce no break
anywhere in the space, at either one or three source centers. Every break, and
every one that survived confirmation on disjoint seeds, lies in concept shift.

\textbf{Concept shift, and why its breaks are not counterexamples.}
Forty configurations were confirmed, and all forty share a property: the
source--target cosine is negative. There the source decision direction points
away from the target's, ERM's target AUROC falls \emph{below} chance ---
between $0.15$ and $0.45$ across the confirmed set --- and an adversarial
method that reaches $0.20$ where ERM reaches $0.15$ is recorded as beating it
by $0.05$. It is not: both rank patients backwards, and the one that does so
slightly less consistently scores higher. AUROC is not a meaningful measure of
transport once the sign has flipped, and the gain it reports there is an
artefact of the metric rather than a recovery of discrimination. At the
benchmark's own concept configuration the verdict is \emph{supported}, with a
gain of $+0.0012$ $[-0.0052, +0.0077]$ and an ERM target AUROC of $0.595$.

Three configurations produced a break with ERM above chance, one in each of
covariate, measurement and concept shift, with gains of $+0.024$, $+0.028$ and
$+0.023$ against the $0.020$ margin. [ESITO DEL CONFIRM MIRATO — inserire.]

\textbf{Limitations.}
Hyperparameters were selected at the default configuration and held fixed
across the sampled space. Where a sampled world departs substantially from that
default --- in particular at high $p/n$, where the covariance shrinkage CORAL
needs is itself a function of that ratio --- the frozen values may be
suboptimal. Since every method including ERM is held fixed under the same
protocol, this affects the precision of individual comparisons rather than
their direction. The sampling space is also our own: it is declared in full and
calibrated to quantities measurable on real cohorts, but a regime outside it
remains outside the claim.
